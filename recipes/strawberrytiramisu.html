Strawberry Tiramisu

Description

Strawberry Tiramisu is a delightful twist on the classic Italian dessert, featuring layers of soft ladyfinger biscuits soaked in strawberry syrup, creamy mascarpone filling, and fresh, juicy strawberries

Light, fruity, and irresistibly smooth, it's the perfect dessert for summer or any special occasion.

Indgredients

  • Ladyfinger biscuits (savoiardi)
  • Fresh strawberries
  • Mascarpone cheese
  • Heavy cream (or whipping cream)
  • Sugar
  • Strawberry syrup or purée
  • Vanilla extract
  1. Prepare the strawberries: Slice the strawberries and mix them with a bit of sugar and lemon juice. Let them sit for about 10 minutes to release their juices.
  2. Make the cream: In a large bowl, whip the heavy cream with sugar and vanilla extract until soft peaks form. Gently fold in the mascarpone cheese until smooth and creamy.
  3. Prepare the dipping liquid: Combine the strawberry syrup (or purée) with a little water if needed to thin it slightly.
  4. Layer the tiramisu: Quickly dip each ladyfinger into the strawberry syrup, making sure not to soak them too long. Arrange a layer of dipped ladyfingers at the bottom of your serving dish. Spread half of the mascarpone mixture over the ladyfingers. Add a layer of sliced strawberries (and a bit of jam if using). Repeat with another layer of dipped ladyfingers, then the remaining mascarpone mixture.
  5. Chill: Cover and refrigerate for at least 4 hours, or overnight, to let the flavors blend and the dessert set.
  6. Serve: Before serving, top with more fresh strawberries and a light dusting of powdered sugar if desired.
